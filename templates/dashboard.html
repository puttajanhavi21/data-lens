How do I read these charts?

A distribution shows how often values fall into different ranges. A correlation close to +1 or −1 suggests a strong linear relationship between numeric columns; correlation alone does not show cause and effect. A missing-values chart highlights columns that may need attention.

What do these model scores mean?

Classification: accuracy is the fraction of correct predictions. Precision measures how often a predicted class is correct; recall measures how many true cases the model finds. F1 balances precision and recall.

Regression: MAE and RMSE measure prediction errors in the target’s units; smaller is better. R² compares the model to predicting the average and can be negative.

Clustering: silhouette score ranges from −1 to 1. Higher values suggest better separated groups. Inertia summarizes distances to cluster centers and usually decreases when you add more clusters.
